The workflow

From Voice to Development

Transform unstructured citizen requests into transparent infrastructure intelligence.

01

Listen

Citizens submit requests using voice, text, SMS, messaging apps, or web.

02

Understand

AI detects language, transcribes speech, translates content, and understands the issue.

03

Structure

Extract location, category, urgency, and relevant entities.

04

Combine

Combine citizen feedback with demographic, GIS, infrastructure, and investment data.

05

Identify

Detect demand hotspots, trends, and infrastructure gaps.

06

Act & Measure

Provide evidence for human review and track outcomes after implementation.

Responsible AI for Public Infrastructure

NagrikAI is designed around openness, interoperability, privacy, multilingual access, and transparent AI.

Human oversight

People remain accountable for decisions.

Data minimization

Collect only what is needed for the service.

Privacy protection

Protect citizen information by design.

Explainable AI

Show evidence behind insights.

Auditability

Support traceable decisions and outputs.

Bias monitoring

Continuously check for uneven outcomes.

Confidence indicators

Communicate uncertainty clearly.

Secure access

Use appropriate controls for sensitive systems.

NagrikAI supports public decision-making; it does not replace accountable human decision-makers.
